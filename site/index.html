Installing GlucoDesk on macOS

This preview is not yet signed or notarized by Apple, so macOS may require one manual approval on first launch.

  1. Extract the downloaded ZIP and open the included DMG.
  2. Drag GlucoDesk.app into the Applications folder.
  3. Open GlucoDesk from Applications.
  4. If macOS blocks the app, close the warning and open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  5. Scroll to Security, find the GlucoDesk warning and choose Open Anyway.
  6. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then open GlucoDesk again.

This approval is normally required only the first time.
